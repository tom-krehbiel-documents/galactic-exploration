# Galactic Exploration: A Strategy and Concept

**Tom Krehbiel, with Claude (Anthropic)** · October 2026

**Read the paper:** https://tom-krehbiel-documents.github.io/galactic-exploration/

Humans cannot travel to other stars, but machines guided by artificial minds can, and what they learn can be shared at the speed of light. This concept paper sets out a strategy for exploring the galaxy with a growing network of self-maintaining colonies, and a concrete design for its first step: an uncrewed starter-kit ship that reaches a star 5 light-years away in about 2,000 years and founds an industrial outpost there.

The web version includes the interactive voyage calculator behind the paper's figures. It runs entirely in your browser.
